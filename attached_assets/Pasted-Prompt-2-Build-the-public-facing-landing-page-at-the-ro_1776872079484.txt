Prompt 2 

Build the public-facing landing page at the / route in the React frontend. This is the primary marketing and conversion page for the personal training business.

Design brief:
- Bold, athletic aesthetic. Dark background (#0A0A0A), electric lime green accent (#C8FF00), white text.
- Typography: Use Google Fonts — "Bebas Neue" for headings, "DM Sans" for body.
- Fully responsive (mobile-first).
- Smooth scroll behaviour throughout.
- Subtle animated entrance effects using CSS keyframes (no heavy libraries).

Sections to build (in order):

1. **Hero Section**
   - Full-viewport height
   - Headline: "RESULTS. NOT EXCUSES." in huge Bebas Neue
   - Sub-headline: "Personal training in Birmingham — online and in-person"
   - Two CTA buttons: "Book a Free Consultation" (primary) + "View Packages" (secondary)
   - Background: dark with a subtle noise/grain texture overlay via CSS

2. **Social Proof Bar**
   - Horizontal strip with 3 stats: "100+ Clients Trained", "4.9★ Average Rating", "13 Years Experience"
   - Background: lime green, black text

3. **Services Section**
   - Three cards: 1-2-1 Personal Training, Online Coaching, Group Training
   - Each card has an icon, title, short description, and a "Learn More" anchor

4. **How It Works**
   - Numbered step-by-step: Free Consultation → Personalised Plan → Train & Track → Get Results
   - Alternating layout (icon left/right) on desktop

5. **Packages Section**
   - Pull packages from GET /api/packages
   - Display as pricing cards with session count, price, and a "Get Started" CTA that opens the enquiry modal

6. **Testimonials**
   - Three static testimonials in quote card format
   - Rotating carousel on mobile

7. **FAQ Section**
   - 6 FAQs as an accordion (expand/collapse)
   - Questions: What areas do you cover? South and central Birmingham, Do you offer online training? How do I get started? What should I bring to a session? Are nutrition plans included? How do I pay?

8. **Lead Capture / Contact Section**
   - Form fields: Name, Email, Phone, Goal (dropdown: Lose Weight, Build Muscle, Improve Fitness, Sport Specific, Other), Message
   - On submit: POST /api/enquiries, show success message, no page reload
   - Include a Google Maps embed placeholder (div with a comment to replace with real embed)

9. **Footer**
   - Logo/name, nav links, social icons (Instagram @jaymacjm, Facebook @Jay PT,), © 2026
